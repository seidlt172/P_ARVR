Assignment 01 - Roll a Ball
Team: Seidl, UrRehman

Minimal version: completed (see seidl_urrehman_minimal.mp4)

Completed bonus tasks (see seidl_urrehman_bonus.mp4): a, b, d, e, f, g, i, j

a) Sound: a short chime plays whenever the Player collects a Pick Up (PickUpSound.cs, generated in code).
b) Maze: 9 thin walls ("Maze Walls" in the scene hierarchy) form a maze inside the play area.
d) Lights: 4 additional lights of different types and colors (warm, magenta and green point lights, blue spot light) in ExtraLights.cs.
e) Soft camera: the camera follows the Player smoothly using Vector3.SmoothDamp (CameraController.cs).
f) Score: the score ("Score: x / y") is shown during gameplay and increases with each collected item (GameHUD.cs).
g) Timer: a 60 second countdown is shown during gameplay; "Time's up!" is displayed when it runs out (GameHUD.cs).
i) "Congratulations!" is displayed when all Pick Ups are collected (GameHUD.cs).
j) Confetti particle system rains down when all Pick Ups are collected (ConfettiCelebration.cs).

Additional notes:
- Press R after the game has ended to restart the scene.
- The HUD, sound, lights and confetti are created by code at runtime (BonusBootstrap.cs), so they appear in the hierarchy as "BonusFeatures" only while the game is running.
